## What is {{PRODUCT_NAME}}?

{{PRODUCT_NAME}} is a Chrome extension that helps businesses monitor social media
platforms for keywords automatically — ideal for finding leads and opportunities.
The tool monitors private and public Facebook groups, Twitter/X, Reddit, LinkedIn,
NextDoor, Telegram, BlueSky, Threads and WhatsApp groups for specified keywords,
then uses AI to read every post and detect buyer intent. {{PRODUCT_NAME}} also
generates personalized responses/comments and content.

### Key Features

- **Social Media Monitoring**: Track keywords across multiple platforms, fully automated
- **AI Lead Detection**: AI analyzes each post for intent and purpose
- **AI Content Creation**: AI generates posts, visual content, and draft comments and responses
- **Post Scheduling**: Schedule content across connected social media accounts
- **Lead Management**: Dashboard to view, filter, and manage found leads
- **Webhook/Zapier Integration**: Export data for further processing, analysis, or custom workflow
- **All Languages Supported**: All languages are supported in keyword tracking, AI responses, and content generation

## Platform-Specific Features

### Facebook

- Monitors public and private groups (you must be a member; you do not need to be an admin)
- Group monitoring limit depends on your plan; additional groups can be added
- Scan interval available from 30 minutes to 8 hours (recommended: 2+ hours)
- **AI Lead Detection**: AI analyzes each post for intent and purpose
- **AI Responses**: AI writes a personalized comment/response. No auto-commenting — a human click is required to send.
- **AI Content Creation**: AI generates posts, visual content, and news headlines
- **Post Scheduling**: Schedule content across connected social media accounts
- All languages supported in keyword tracking, AI responses, and content generation

### LinkedIn

- Monitors posts from within and outside your network (up to 3+ degree connections)
- Company pages and personal profiles (including Top Voices)
- Does not monitor comments
- AI-generated responses from within the extension
- Faster scan intervals possible (30+ minutes)
- Scrape more posts by changing the settings (default settings are recommended)
- Monitor specific occupations posting about your keywords
- Filter leads by job title (e.g., CEO, CTO, CMO)
- Webhook data export supported

### Twitter/X

- Location options: scan posts "Near Me" or "Global"
- Faster scan intervals possible (30+ minutes)
- Detect posts by influencers for your keywords
- AI-generated responses from within the extension. No auto-DMs, to protect your account.
- Webhook data export supported
- Fully automated; runs 12 times a day by default (frequency can be increased)

## Monitoring Configuration

### Keywords

- Unlimited keywords supported (ideal: 8–10).
- Supports 3-word phrases maximum
- Each keyword must be 3 or more characters

- **Optimization tips:**
  - Use variations (e.g., "senior developer", "senior", "developer")
  - Include singular/plural forms
  - Add industry jargon and common misspellings
  - Review and update regularly
  - By default, keywords use exact match. You can switch to "contains" so any post
    containing the word is shown.

### Scanning Settings

- **Path:** Settings > Scan & Keyword Settings > Scan Settings
- **Frequency options:** automated scan every 30 min, 1 hr, 2 hrs, 4 hrs, 6 hrs, or 8 hrs.
  Manual on-demand scan available anytime.
- **Recommendation:** 2+ hours for Facebook

## Data Management & Privacy

### Data Storage, Retention & Privacy

- All data is stored locally in your browser (not on servers)
- All leads/posts are automatically deleted after 30 days unless saved
- Changing devices requires a complete re-setup
- No server-side data storage (for compliance reasons)
- Users are responsible for platform terms compliance
- Data is not synced when changing devices/browsers
- To delete all data, uninstall the browser extension (not recoverable)
- **Restore option:** available during new-device setup; restores only keywords,
  groups, and business details — no leads or posts

### Data Export

- **Option 1:** Export/import all your data (social media posts) from extension settings, go to Settings > Business Info & Account Management > Backup, Restore & Data control > Export Data.
- **Option 2:** No direct API, but the Zapier API can be used to export and process
  data systematically
- Webhook support for real-time export

### Data & Account Deletion

- Uninstall the browser extension. This removes all your data and social media
  connections permanently. No social media data is stored on servers.
- When you cancel your subscription, your billing account and details are also removed.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### No Leads Found

1. Ensure keywords are set to "ALL" (not a specific keyword)
2. Verify platform connections
3. Check that Facebook groups are selected
4. Confirm an appropriate scan interval
5. Allow at least 72 hours of monitoring

### Connection Issues

1. Update your browser
2. Log out and back in to your social media accounts
3. Reconnect accounts to the extension
4. Reload the extension: Settings > Debug & Data Control > Click to Reload

### Login Issues

- Check spam folders for the magic link
- Confirm you entered the correct email during signup
- Click the magic link from the same device where login was requested
- Wait a few minutes to receive the email

### Facebook Account Warnings

- Increase the time between scan intervals (from 30 minutes to 2 hours)
- Maintain regular Facebook activity outside the extension
- New social profiles need to warm up
- Do not comment a lot; increase comment activity slowly
- If monitoring many groups, reduce them and increase gradually

### Wrong Profile

The extension uses the social media session already logged into your browser.
Go to the relevant platform, log in with the correct profile, return to the
extension, and refresh the page — it will pick up the correct account.

### Debug Connection Tools

Go to Settings > Business Info & Account Management > Debug & Data Control

- Platform-specific debugging
- Individual data cleaner
- Extension reload option

## Limitations

- No automatic commenting/messaging — only automatic comment drafts; human review
  and click required to comment
- No geo-targeting for Facebook posts (join location-based groups instead). Twitter
  supports "nearby"; other platforms have their own location settings.
- No team/multi-user access to the same account unless passwords are shared (not recommended)
- No historical data search (current/future posts only)
- One account per set of social profiles. Use Chrome profiles and an additional
  subscription to monitor with multiple social media accounts.

## Product Guide & How-To's

> Screenshot links below point to original-brand assets — replace with your own captures.

- **Dashboard overview:** See the number of leads found and pending outreach from your Dashboard.
- **Change extension language:** Go to the sidebar and click on "English", then select another language.
- **Hide the menu bar:** Click the arrow next to the logo to hide the menu bar for more screen space.
- **Top performing groups:** Go to your Dashboard and scroll down to see your top groups.
- **Manage billing:** Open the menu at the top-right corner and click on Billing.

### Leads & Outreach

- **Hide keywords from the Outreach screen:** Click "Show less" for more space.
- **Buying-intent only:** Click the "Buying Intent" filter on the Leads & Outreach screen.
- **Filter by platform:** On the Leads & Outreach screen, select a platform icon at the top.
- **Facebook group leads:** Select the "Facebook Groups" tab to edit keywords, modify groups, and view leads.
- **Reddit leads:** Select the "Reddit" tab to edit keywords, modify subreddits, and view leads.
- **Subreddit-only posts:** On the "Reddit" tab, select "Subreddit Community".
- **Global Reddit posts:** On the "Reddit" tab, select "Anywhere".
- **Qualify & contact a lead:** Browse the "Leads & Outreach" screen and click "Comment" to start outreach.
- **Comment on a lead's post:** Click "Comment", write your text or use AI to auto-reply, then click "Add Comment".
- **Edit the auto-reply prompt:** Click "Prompt" and edit it to match your needs.
- **Saved messages:** Click "Comment" > "Saved Messages" to save reusable replies.
- **Block a user:** Click "Block" on the lead square to never see them again.
- **Edit subreddits:** On the "Reddit" tab, click "Edit subreddits".
- **Filter leads by sentiment/group/subreddit:** Click the "Filters" button on the right.
- **Manage blocked users:** Go to Filters > Manage Blocked.
- **Brand & competitor mentions:** Go to the "Mentions" tab and select the brand to monitor.
- **X influencers / Global vs local:** Go to the "X" tab to find influencers and set Global or local monitoring.

### Schedule Posts

- **Create & publish posts:** Go to the "Schedule Posts" tab; edit the prompt, regenerate, or create a post/story.
- **Modify the posts prompt:** Click "Prompt", edit your instructions, and select the categories you want.
- **Share AI news:** Go to "Schedule Posts" > "AI News", edit your news keywords, or select a piece of news to share.
- **View published posts/drafts/errors:** Go to the "Published" tab.
- **Boost reach:** Add the news link as the first comment to prevent the algorithm from hiding your post.
- **Customize a post:** Edit the caption, remove text from the image, select multiple images, choose platforms or Facebook groups, and set the time to post.
